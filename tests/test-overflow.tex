%% OVERFLOW regression — a band at a large Y must not spill past the text area.
%%
%% A single band low on the page (Y=420pt) leaves a short below-band region. Before
%% the fix the assembled page was built as \vbox to\textheight with the overfull
%% signal suppressed (\vfuzz\maxdimen) and a phantom \baselineskip of interline glue
%% before the post-band row — so the below-band text printed ~8pt past the bottom of
%% the text area instead of paginating. The fix bounds the page to \@colht, suppresses
%% that phantom glue, reserves the row depths, and stops hiding overfull.
%%
%% ASSERTION: the log must contain no "Overfull \vbox" (\vfuzz is a 2pt line-
%% quantization tolerance, far below the ~8pt spill this guards against). Add
%% \usepackage{showframe} to see the text-area boundary while eyeballing.
