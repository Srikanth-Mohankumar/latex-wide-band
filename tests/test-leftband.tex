%% LEFT-COLUMN (column-1) BAND sample.
%%
%% The \begin{wideband} is authored after paragraph [02], so with a low split
%% height Y the band lands within the *left* column of the two-column flow. The
%% same in-run rebuild that handles a right-column band handles this one: on the
%% band page both finished columns are recombined and re-split at Y, the band is
%% set full width across the page, and both columns flow around it in reading
%% order. This shows wideband-cc is one general mechanism for either column, not
%% a col-1-only special case.
