%% preceding text — falls in the *right* column of the two-column flow. A genuine
%% right-column band cannot stay at its source position on the same page (the
%% impossibility theorem), so wideband-cc places it by DETACH-UP: the band is
%% pulled up to a chosen height Y and set full width, and both columns flow around

%% column, which is the whole reason a full-width band is wanted.
