%% rebuilt here. Load wideband-cc AFTER such packages.
%%
%% Here flushend is loaded before wideband-cc; page 1 carries a band, page 2 is the
%% short last page. flushend must still balance page 2's two columns.
%%
%% ASSERTION: FLUSHEND-BAND on page 1; on page 2 (the last page) the two columns end
%% within one line of each other (lowest baselines within 13pt) — before the fix the
%% left column ran full and the right held only the remainder; no "Overfull \vbox";

%% =====================================================================
